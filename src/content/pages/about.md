Hi, I am **Lan Luo (罗澜)**, an undergraduate student at East China Normal University.

My interests lie in **spatial artificial intelligence (GeoAI)**, **embodied intelligence**, and **multimodal systems**. I am interested in how intelligent systems perceive and understand the world, connect language with spatial information, and turn understanding into action.

## Research Interests

- **Spatial AI and UAV systems:** environment understanding, vision-language navigation, and intelligence in urban spaces.
- **Embodied and multimodal intelligence:** connecting perception and language with action.
- **Research engineering:** reproducing experiments, debugging systems, and building evaluation workflows.

## About This Blog

I use this space to keep reading notes, document experiments, and reflect on what I learn while building things. Some posts are finished essays; others are working notes that I revisit as my understanding develops.

The site is primarily in English. Blog posts may be written in English or Chinese.
